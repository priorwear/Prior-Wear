NEED TO KNOW

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

HOW DO I PLACE AN ORDER?

Select a product, click Order Now, fill in your details and press Send Order to WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens with a prefilled message; you must send it yourself.

ARE THE PRICES IN US DOLLARS?

Yes. The sample prices on this demo store are displayed in USD. Replace them with your actual selling prices before publishing.

HOW DO I CHOOSE MY SIZE AND COLOR?

Select your preferred options in the order form. Confirm actual product availability with the seller before finalizing your order.

DO YOU SHIP INTERNATIONALLY?

International delivery availability, shipping charges, customs duties and delivery times must be confirmed with PRIOR WEAR before you advertise worldwide shipping.

WHEN IS MY ORDER CONFIRMED?

Sending a WhatsApp request does not automatically confirm your order. The seller must confirm stock, final price, shipping and payment arrangements.

WHAT PAYMENT METHODS ARE AVAILABLE?

Payment methods should be agreed with the seller. This demo does not process card payments or collect online payments.
